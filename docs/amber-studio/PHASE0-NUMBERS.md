answers** — I'd rather drop a line than ship a guess.

Two things per project: a **date** and a **number**.

- **Date** → the `modified` column in the Finder window. ISO `2026-03-14`, or just
  `2026-03` if that's all you've got. The month is enough to beat `2025 – 2026`.
- **Number** → one fact with a figure in it, about what the thing *did*, not what it is.
  Users, bookings, donations, schools, containers, listings, staff, countries, load time
  on a bad connection. One good one beats three vague ones.

I've put the kind of number that would land hardest for each. Ignore my suggestion if you
have a better one.
